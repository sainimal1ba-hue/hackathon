# Design War — The Emperor's Verdict

*Three design gladiators fought for the commercial face of HEROKILL. The Emperor judged them on six axes, 0–10 each (total /60): WOW · BRAND · BUILD24 · DIFF · CRED · FIT. Ground truth: a commercial website for users across the internet — "a billion dollar company made it, Awwwards style" — built in 24 hours by 4–5 people, for a product whose demo is a live death and resurrection, in front of student judges and a voting crowd of ~150 rival clans.*

**One-line notes**
- **VAULT** — The most honest engineer in the arena, and the most invisible: a flawless system with a settings page for a soul.
- **GREENROOM** — The only gladiator guaranteed to ship smiling by hour 20; the crowd would adopt the mascot and forget the murder.
- **OPERA** — The only one who understood the brief: not "design a website" but "stage a death two hundred people feel in their chests."

## Kill lines for the fallen
- **VAULT, you fall by your own blade:** forty clans will ship your palette tonight. You designed the monitor, not the execution. Credibility without spectacle earns a judge's nod — never a crowd's roar.
- **GREENROOM, you fall warm:** your mascot is lovable, but this product's demo is a murder. The audience came for blood. Warmth can explain death; it cannot perform it. Stripe already built your site — with four hundred engineers.

## The decisive argument for THE OPERA
AUDIENCE VOTE decides the crown, and WOW, DIFF and FIT are the crowd's axes — OPERA sweeps all three: no rival makes a student die in ten seconds, execute the kill themselves (JUDGE'S TRIGGER), and count the resurrection aloud at 00:07. OPERA's only wounds — BUILD24 and CRED — are engineering problems, and engineering can be adopted. Nobody can adopt the roar.

## Adopted from the fallen (now law)
From **VAULT**: the deterministic data spine (pre-recorded JSON event stream; WebSockets only if the wire is live); CSS-only scrubber mechanics; typed.js terminal; the 800ms confirm-hold trigger; JetBrains Mono as the machine's laconic lowercase voice (no exclamation marks, ever); the hour-by-hour build order.
From **GREENROOM**: green-lock theater (fake browser chrome + padlock beat) inside THE PUSH; the accessibility law (color + icon + label; prefers-reduced-motion; arrow-key scrubber); IntersectionObserver entrances — no scroll library; exit-code translations on certificates, written for screenshots.
