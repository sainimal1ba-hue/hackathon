## Battle structure — three rounds

| Round | Name | What happens | What decides it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Build Your Base | Online: submit a **10-slide idea deck** (anonymous, no account needed) | Shortlisting to ~120–150 clans |
| 1 | The Raid | 24h offline build at VIT Chennai | Clan War I judging → **top 8 advance** |
| 2 | Arena Finals | Offline finale: Clan War II + Crown Match | **AUDIENCE VOTE decides the champions** |

## Critical implications for strategy

1. **The 10-slide deck is the gate.** No deck, no Raid. The warplan must convert directly into 10 slides.
2. **The final crown is an AUDIENCE VOTE.** The demo must win a live crowd, not just judges. Theatrical demo moments are endgame strategy, not decoration.
3. **~120–150 rival clans** — generic builds drown in the noise. The angle must be "the obvious build + something nobody else will think of".
4. Shortlisted clans register on the institute portal, join the WhatsApp group, and pick their problem statement there.
