# PS-AI-01 — VOX NOCTURNA

**Problem:** Offline Voice-to-Action System Controller — AI & Machine Learning track
*Gladiator battle speech — Round 1: Topic War*

---

## CLAIM
- **Feature surface:** wake-word → transcribe → plan → act → verify → undo is five products in one trench — every layer independently demoable.
- **24H feasibility:** zero training. Whisper.cpp, Vosk, Ollama, PyAutoGUI are off-the-shelf parts — this is a glue-code war, and gluing is this team's native craft.
- **Demo wow:** we unplug the router on stage and the machine still obeys. The crown is an audience vote, and audiences vote for the dead cable held aloft.
- **Differentiation vs. crowd:** the lazy majority ships chatbots that *talk*; we ship one that *acts, verifies, and undoes* — collision-proof even if thirty clans pick voice.
- **Team fit:** Python glue (in reach), Node dashboard (home turf), action engine behind 98 regression tests. Rivals demo vibes; we demo CI.

## THE BUILD
1. **Unplugged opener:** router killed live — "Nox: open the project, rename these 30 screenshots by date, zip them, show the folder." It obeys.
2. **Plan view:** Ollama turns free speech into a step DAG on a dashboard — the crowd watches the machine *think*.
3. **Dry-run mode:** click-map overlay of intended actions, voice-approved before execution.
4. **Verify loop:** after every step, screen-vision confirms the click landed; miss → auto-retry with adjusted coordinates.
5. **Undo journal:** "Nox, undo that." Every action reversible.
6. **Voice macros:** "Remember this as morning routine" — compound actions recorded, replayed by one word.
7. **Permission tiers + latency panel:** destructive commands gated; all-local, milliseconds displayed.

## KILL
- **PS-AI-02:** blank-weights training in 24h yields a loss curve, not a product. You'll present TensorBoard; we'll present a machine obeying commands with the internet dead. Audiences vote for magic, not metrics.
- **PS-WEB-03:** 60 FPS + CRDT + WebRTC is three hard problems; one dropped frame on stage and your demo is a stuttering apology. Perf-tuning under a countdown is how clans die.
- **PS-BLK-02:** no prior chain deployments on this team, and ZK verification is invisible — you cannot *show* math to a crowd. Worse, three blockchain PS split that audience three ways.

## HEEL
Stage hardware: mic noise, Ollama RAM, one UI hiccup breaking a click. Survival: quantized models pre-baked into a Docker demo image (our CI weapon), a rehearsed fixed demo flow, and the verify-loop self-healing missed clicks. Fallback chain: Whisper.cpp → Vosk; Ollama-3B → rule-based grammar.

## FINAL BLOW
When the router dies on that stage and our machine keeps working, the crowd forgets every slide they ever saw — and the crown follows the memory.
