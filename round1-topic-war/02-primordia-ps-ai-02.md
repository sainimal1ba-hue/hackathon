## CLAIM
- **Absolute feasibility in an offline arena.** A lean network built from scratch trains in minutes on a laptop CPU. No testnet, no APIs, no downloads — the only AI project with zero external dependencies. *(24h feasibility)*
- **The performance IS the birth.** A model evolving from random noise to correctly decoding live signals is intuitive, dramatic theater. *(Demo wow)*
- **No fear of the crowd.** "From scratch" terrifies API-calling clans; PS-AI-01 and PS-AI-03 will be the most crowded lanes — almost none survive here. *(Crowd risk)*
- **Our discipline is our superpower.** CI + test discipline becomes seeded, reproducible training runs with an accuracy regression gate — credentials no rival can fake. The dashboard IS the full-stack product. *(Team fit)*
- **Endless feature runway.** Training, visualization, stress-testing, compression — each stage an independent demoable module. *(Feature surface)*

## THE BUILD
1. **Blank stage** — start training live in front of judges: loss curve diving, weight heatmaps crystallizing.
2. **Intelligence timelapse** — rapid evolution from gibberish to correct spectrogram decoding.
3. **Signal lab** — live FFT/filter pipeline; feed microphone audio, get beautiful spectra.
4. **Stress chamber** — feed noise and adversarial inputs; show robustness under attack.
5. **Convergence race** — two architectures side by side; audience votes live for the winner.
6. **Brain scanner** — Grad-CAM activation maps revealing what the model "sees".
7. **Minimalist mode** — quantization/pruning compressing the model live.
8. **CI for AI** — GitHub Actions re-runs training, flags any accuracy drop.

## KILL
- **PS-AI-01:** in a noisy hall with ambient mic noise, STT accuracy collapses — before even counting the integration hell of offline Whisper/Vosk/Ollama. And it's assembled, not built. Most-picked and most likely to die.
- **PS-AI-03:** nondeterministic agents loop on stage, failures are opaque, and debugging LangGraph chaos in 5 minutes is impossible.
- **PS-BLK-01/02:** on an offline stage, verification networks collapse — no internet, no chain reputation.

## HEEL
A from-scratch model cannot beat GPT; the audience may think it's "just charts". Survival: don't show a static dashboard — show every step of every evolution. The demo is not the result, but the process of learning itself. With a lean spec, one laptop suffices.

## FINAL BLOW
Others can only show software that USES intelligence — we will BIRTH intelligence live in front of 200 voting spectators.
