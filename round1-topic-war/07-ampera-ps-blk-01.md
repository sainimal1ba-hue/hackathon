# PS-BLK-01 — AMPERA

**Problem:** Blockchain-Powered Battery Passport & Circularity Network — Blockchain & Web3 track
*Gladiator battle speech — Round 1: Topic War*

---

## CLAIM
- **Feature surface:** one passport = birth certificate, service log, custody chain, tamper alarm, second-life marketplace, recycling certificate — no rival matches that feature count.
- **Feasibility + team fit:** the chain runs locally on Hardhat — Solidity is just typed backend code. Ours is the only arena where running your test suite *live* is a demo feature; our 98-test CI discipline becomes theater.
- **Demo wow:** the only problem with a physical prop — tap a battery, its whole life materializes on screen. Audiences vote for what they can touch.
- **Crowd risk:** the herd stampedes to the three AI lanes; blockchain terrifies clans. We own our lane almost alone.
- **Differentiation:** rivals ship ledgers; we add counterfeit drama and the closed circularity loop — and EU battery passports become law in Feb 2027, so we demo compliance infrastructure, not a toy.

## THE BUILD
1. **Birth:** mint passport with cell origin and cobalt provenance; QR + NFC sticker on a real battery pack prop.
2. **Tap-to-passport:** phone tap → lifecycle dashboard: health %, cycles, custody trail, carbon.
3. **Live custody transfer:** battery handed across the stage; ownership moves on chain; explorer updates instantly.
4. **Tamper theater:** scan a relabeled counterfeit → screaming red TAMPERED alert on hash mismatch.
5. **Service attestations:** service-center role logs health checks; warranty and insurance verifier mode.
6. **Second-life marketplace:** retired EV battery listed for home storage; circularity score, live carbon-avoided counter.
7. **Rebirth:** recycler closes the loop, issues certificate, materials re-enter supply — full circle on screen.
8. **Audit mode:** regulator view plus our test suite attacking the contract live — double-spend rejected, green lights.

## KILL
- **PS-AI-02:** 24 hours training blank weights on hackathon laptops yields a loss curve, not a demo — no convergence, no wow, and the AI herd tramples itself.
- **PS-WEB-02:** "crash-proof, resumable, never duplicates" — you cannot theatrically demo the *absence* of failure in five minutes. Our wow is one tap long.
- **PS-BLK-02:** our sibling, still dead: offline ZK with zero crypto experience is research-grade, and ZK math is invisible on stage. Our verification is a tap the whole hall understands.

## HEEL
Never deployed a chain. Survival: local Hardhat — no mainnet, no gas, no wallets to fumble; contracts stay minimal (mint, transfer, attest, recycle), all logic lives in the web layer we rule. NFC may fail — QR fallback rehearsed. And our weakness becomes armor: proven test discipline is the one trait that ships safe smart contracts in 24 hours.

## FINAL BLOW
Every rival shows pixels; AMPERA hands the audience a battery and lets them hold the truth — and the crowd votes for the demo it can touch.
