## CLAIM
- **Feature surface:** issuer portal, holder wallet, verifier kiosk, ZK selective disclosure, offline QR verification, revocation, social recovery — eight distinct demo-ables (Criterion 1).
- **24h feasibility:** DID/VC is signed JSON plus vetted libraries — no chain, no ML training, no native mobile. Pure full-stack web; our home turf (Criteria 2, 6).
- **Demo wow:** we kill the Wi-Fi on stage and the green check lives. That is the crown moment (Criterion 3).
- **Crowd risk:** it sounds mathematically terrifying, so the 150 clans flee to AI prompts. The humanitarian angle owns the audience vote (Criteria 4, 5).

## THE BUILD
1. NGO issuer portal — issues an "Education Credential" as a signed W3C VC QR.
2. Refugee wallet — accountless PWA; credentials survive via encrypted QR backup.
3. Selective disclosure — "over 18, certified nurse" — never reveals name, birthdate, or camp location.
4. Offline verifier kiosk — camera scan in airplane mode; green check.
5. Tamper theater — edit one QR character live on stage; instant red **FALSIFIED**.
6. Revocation — issuer revokes stolen credentials; caught offline via signed status list.
7. Social recovery — 3-of-5 guardian re-issuance flow.
8. CI badge on screen — crypto flows locked under our 98-test discipline.

## KILL
- **PS-AI-01:** live mic + accent noise + 24h = demo roulette, and it is one feature. We show eight.
- **PS-AI-02:** blank-weight training yields a loss curve and a toy. Nobody votes for a loss curve.
- **PS-BLK-01:** battery passport demands NFC hardware, fake supply-chain actors, and a chain you've never deployed. We deploy **zero chains** — did:key needs no ledger, no gas, no risk.

## HEEL
We have never shipped ZK; hand-rolled crypto kills. Day-zero, hour-one: vetted libraries (did:key, Ed25519, BBS+/AnonCreds). If true ZKP bites within 24h, honest fallback to salted selective disclosure — the airplane-mode demo survives either way. Our testing culture is the armor.

## FINAL BLOW
When the internet dies on stage and identity lives, a thousand people will remember one thing: credentials that survive when everything else is lost.
