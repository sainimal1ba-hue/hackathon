*Gladiator battle speech — Round 1: Topic War*

## CLAIM
- **Feature surface:** fraud forensics is a feature machine — explorer, scoring, tracing, clustering, alerts, case files. Eight-plus distinct demo-ables from one codebase.
- **24h feasibility:** We never WRITE a smart contract — we READ public ledgers through free Etherscan/RPC endpoints. That's a scraping, caching, aggregation problem: our home turf. Zero blockchain experience required — the only blockchain PS where that's true.
- **Demo wow:** Paste a real heist wallet, watch stolen money flow across chains in an animated graph, slam out a court-ready evidence file. The audience votes for a thriller, not a dashboard.
- **Differentiation + crowd risk:** Blockchain scares the herd — they stampede to AI. The few who dare this PS will ship single-chain Etherscan clones; cross-chain hops, entity clustering, and forensic replay are open ground.
- **Team fit:** risk-scoring rules, address parsing, edge cases — deterministic, testable logic our 98-test/CI/Docker discipline devours alive.

## THE BUILD
1. **Wallet X-ray** — paste address → 0–100 risk score with factor breakdown.
2. **Live fund-flow graph** — animated money movement, fully interactive.
3. **"Follow the money" replay** — peel chains lighting up hop by hop, heist-movie style.
4. **Cross-chain hop tracing** — ETH → bridge → Tron USDT cash-out.
5. **Entity clustering** — co-spend heuristics merging scammer wallets into one criminal.
6. **Watchlists + live alerts** on flagged addresses.
7. **Case file builder** — court-ready PDF evidence export for law enforcement.
8. **Time-travel forensics** — scrub the chain's timeline like a DVR.

## KILL
- **PS-FIN-03:** My closest twin — but it demands graph neural networks we cannot train, running on simulated data nobody believes. We run on REAL stolen money from REAL famous hacks.
- **PS-BLK-02:** Zero blockchain experience plus ZK proofs in 24h is cryptographic suicide. Its demo is a QR scan; ours is a manhunt.

## HEEL
We have no blockchain experience, and bridge APIs are messy, rate-limited, stage-fragile. Survival plan: we're read-only (no Solidity), demo on BTC/ETH/Tron with Dockerized cache snapshots as fallback, score against a curated known-scam dataset, and our CI muscle means the demo runs at hour 24 — not just hour 4.
