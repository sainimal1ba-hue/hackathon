# PS-FIN-01 — LEDGERKEEPER

**Problem:** Intelligent Financial Management Ecosystem — FinTech & Business Innovation track
*Gladiator battle speech — Round 1: Topic War*

---

## CLAIM
- **Feature surface:** Money is infinite demo-able features — budgets, allowances, approvals, forecasts, invoices, alerts — all browser-renderable, all live in one product.
- **24h feasibility:** This is CRUD-plus-logic in our exact stack — no training runs, no testnet gas, no NAT traversal. We ship, not slide.
- **Team fit:** Ledgers must BALANCE — our 98-test CI discipline becomes a product feature: the only finance demo that cannot drift into negative money.
- **Demo wow:** The crown is an audience vote — and everyone in that hall has a family, a wallet, or a dream shop.
- **Crowd risk:** Gladiators chase AI and blockchain flash. The "boring" ledger is underpicked — and we out-polish whoever dares.

## THE BUILD
1. **Multi-tier vault** — personal/family/business workspaces under one login; live role-switch, owner → child, permissions changing on screen.
2. **Allowance rails** — auto-split ₹500 to kids on payday; kid taps "can I buy this?" — parent approves live on stage.
3. **Envelope budgets** — rule-engine auto-categorization, zero ML, drag-and-drop envelopes that fill and burst.
4. **Cash-flow time machine** — 90-day forecast slider; "what-if" toggles ("client pays late") move the doom-date live.
5. **Business mode** — receivables aging + runway countdown: "47 days of cash left."
6. **Alert engine** — subscription creep, grocery-spike detection.
7. **Audit theater** — mutate one transaction on stage; the test suite catches the imbalance in front of everyone. CI as a feature.

## KILL
- **PS-AI-02:** A from-scratch model in 24h with no ML infra is a parrot that outputs mush — your demo IS the model, and it will embarrass you live.
- **PS-BLK-02:** Zero chain deployments behind you; ZK proofs don't render in a browser. Your five minutes are loading spinners ending in an RPC error — the audience votes for what they can SEE.
- **PS-WEB-03:** 60 FPS multiplayer over venue Wi-Fi you don't control. One congested router, and your crown dies on latency.

## HEEL
Honest: "budget app" sounds tame, and several clans will drift toward finance — but they'll ship spreadsheets with charts. We ship a story — one family, one shop, one dashboard — with polish they can't match. Offline venue, no bank APIs? A pre-seeded realistic dataset, tested green before doors open.

## FINAL BLOW
Every other clan demos technology; we demo the audience's own life — and people vote for themselves.
