## CLAIM
- **Feature surface (C1):** A validation engine is a feature factory — idea scoring, pricing lab, burn clock, scenario simulator, investor memo. Ten-plus screens, every one demo-able, none filler.
- **24h feasibility (C2 + C6):** Monte Carlo is deterministic math + charts — zero model training, zero exotic infra. Our 98-test CI discipline means the numbers on stage are *provably correct*, which no rival can claim.
- **Demo wow (C3):** The audience watches 10,000 simulated startups live and die in real time, then a verdict stamp slams down: SURVIVE or DEAD. That's theater.
- **Differentiation (C4):** Everyone builds the obvious dashboard; nobody thinks to add a hostile AI investor that attacks your idea mid-pitch using the sim's own numbers.
- **Crowd risk (C5):** The herd chases voice assistants, blockchains, and fraud GNNs. "Simulation engine" sounds like homework — thin crowd, wide open lane.

## THE BUILD
1. **Idea Autopsy** — paste a startup idea; instant scorecard (market, moat, founder-fit).
2. **Monte Carlo Arena** — 10,000 parallel futures race live into survival curves.
3. **Pricing Lab** — drag price/CAC sliders; LTV heatmap repaints instantly.
4. **Burn Clock** — runway countdown under live-adjustable spend.
5. **Scenario Weapons** — presets: Funding Winter, Competitor Clone, Google Enters.
6. **Red-Team Investor (AI)** — brutal VC persona that interrogates the idea citing sim data.
7. **One-click Investor Memo** — PDF with tornado sensitivity chart.
8. **Kill Score Leaderboard** — audience submits ideas live; the engine ranks their fate.

## KILL
- **PS-AI-02:** 24 hours, blank weights, zero ML infra — you present a loss curve to 150 non-ML students. No theater, no crown.
- **PS-BLK-02:** ZK verification for displaced people — noble, undemoable. No chain experience plus testnet latency on stage means a frozen cursor at minute four.
- **PS-WEB-03:** 60fps CRDT sync consumes three engineers' entire 24h just to avoid lag, and the wow *requires a second human cooperating perfectly*. My wow runs solo, seeded, reproducibly — RNG seeded, so the demo literally cannot fail.

## HEEL
"AI" must be earned: Monte Carlo alone won't satisfy the AI framing. Fix — the red-team investor LLM and narrative generation sit atop the deterministic engine; AI is the cast, math is the stage. Second heel: finance jargon bores crowds — we translate everything into life-or-death language: *funding winter, kill score, ten thousand futures*.

## FINAL BLOW
Rivals will demo tools — we demo **fate itself**, and an audience that just watched its own startup ideas live or die on our screen votes for the engine that judged them.
