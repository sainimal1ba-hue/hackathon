## CLAIM
- **Feature surface (W):** A real-time fraud net is not one feature — it's a firehose, a live graph, an alert engine, a scoring engine, a case file, and an action console. Eight demo-able surfaces, one coherent story.
- **24h feasibility:** Detection wins with graph algorithms — community detection, fan-in/fan-out, velocity features — plus lightweight scoring, all in Python libs within our reach. Zero training runs. Our 98-test discipline means it doesn't die on stage.
- **Demo WOW:** Money moving across a glowing graph, a fraud ring flaring red mid-stream, one click freezing it. The AUDIENCE sees the crime happen and sees us stop it. That's theater.
- **Crowd risk:** The herd stampedes to voice agents, multi-agent AI, and blockchain. FIN-03 is a narrower gate with a wide moat — and rivals who attempt it will chase deep learning and ship nothing.
- **Team fit:** Real-time systems are event loops, WebSockets, and pipelines — full-stack muscle. And a risk-scoring engine is exactly the kind of code you wrap in tests and CI.

## THE BUILD
1. **Live transaction firehose** — thousands of synthetic txns/sec feeding a glowing force-directed graph.
2. **Fraud ring detection** — coordinated communities light up red with blast-radius highlighting.
3. **Live risk scoring** — 0–100 per account, updating with reasons: velocity, 3AM bursts, new counterparties.
4. **Anomaly alerts** — structuring/splits, mule fan-in/fan-out patterns.
5. **Investigator war room** — click any node: dossier, neighborhood, and an explainability card ("why was this flagged").
6. **Attack replay** — scrub the timeline, replay a full fraud campaign in 10 seconds.
7. **Auto-actions** — freeze card, step-up verification, block merchant — with an audit trail.
8. **Tuning sliders** — threshold knobs the audience can play with live.
