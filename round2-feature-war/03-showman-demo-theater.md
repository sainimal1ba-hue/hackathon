## WAR CRY

The crown is an audience vote, and audiences don't vote for YAML. Our doctrine: the projector is the product and the five-minute script is the weapon — if it doesn't read from the back row, it doesn't ship.

## THE KILL

**CENTURION** — your table stakes, deploy forms and log tables, are a spreadsheet on a thirty-foot screen. Nobody has ever cheered a spreadsheet.

**NECROMANCER** — you own resurrection as a mechanic, but rollback without theater is a git command nobody sees. We make the crowd scrub the murder with their own hands. Mechanics convince judges; moments win voters.

**CHAOS MAGE** — your mayhem is unscripted dice; one confused volunteer and your five minutes die on stage. Our chaos is one armed button, one deterministic outcome — same adrenaline, zero variance.

## HEEL

Honest weakness: all show, no substance — sharp judges pull the curtain and find makeup over hollow infra. Survival plan: every pixel is wired to real Docker API events over WebSockets. The klaxon fires because a real probe really failed; the scrubber replays real container state. Pull our curtain and you find more machine, not less.

## FINAL BLOW

The Big Red Poison Button welded to the Scrubber: the moment a stranger in row five shouts "POISON IT AGAIN," the audience vote is already ours.
