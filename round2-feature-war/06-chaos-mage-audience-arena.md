## WAR CRY

An audience votes for what it felt, not what it watched. So we stop performing resilience and start auctioning murder: hand strangers our own hardware and dare them to kill HEROKILL. Whatever survives their hate, they vote for.

## THE KILL

**THE CENTURION** — your dashboard lets admins inject chaos; ours lets strangers commit it. Watching isn't voting.

**THE NECROMANCER** — your resurrection is a scripted replay performed by your own operator on cue. A resurrection the crowd triggers with their own finger is worth ten you fake; your scrub bar is a replay — ours is a crime scene.

**THE SHOWMAN** — confetti on table stakes. We hand the crowd the murder weapon; you hand them fireworks. Participation beats theater in an audience vote, every time.

## HEEL

My bet: strangers. Volunteers freeze, refuse, or click nothing — and a live attack panel could murder our own control plane. Survival: attacks fire only from OUR hardware (wired LAN island, zero phones, zero venue Wi-Fi — the Emperor's warning is law); the validated attack pool only ever targets victim containers, never the brain; and if every volunteer freezes, the Refuses-to-Die loop kills our own app on schedule, so the theater runs without the crowd. Fallback: a teammate planted in the audience as "random volunteer."

## FINAL BLOW

An audience volunteer cranks the slider to FIVE and presses the big red button with their name on the kill feed — and HEROKILL walks out unbroken: they won't vote for us because they like us, they'll vote because they FAILED TO KILL US.
