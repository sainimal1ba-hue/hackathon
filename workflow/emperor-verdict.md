## KILL LINES

- **Railway's killing stroke:** "Every 2 a.m. crash already died in CI at home." A machine that rehearses nightly, murders premeditated, certs pre-minted, and a dead-man switch that revives any beat in 60 seconds. It converted the 24 hours into theater before the doors opened.
- **Railway's exposed throat:** "If it wasn't rehearsed, it doesn't exist" is a vow of helplessness when the arena invents a failure you never scripted. A fixed timetable is a row of dominoes; its people were drilled in *obeying*, not *deciding*. FLEX 4 — the lowest score ever survived.
- **War Room's killing stroke:** Gates that *move*, decisions drilled every two hours, a board where "done means demo-able" and blocked cards get a body in ten minutes. The finest small-clan human engineering in the arena.
- **War Room's exposed throat:** It arrives at a 24h *offline* war with nothing frozen, no pre-baked images, no cert, no DNS kit, no dress rehearsal — burning its first six hours manufacturing what the Railway carried in a backpack.

## CHAMPION — WF-1 THE RAILWAY, 48–47

Offline hours cannot be minted, and the crowd votes only for what lands *every time*. The Railway owns both: pre-baking makes H0–2 a formality; CI turns nights into rehearsals; the switch guards the finale. The War Room's superiority exists only *when the plan breaks* — but most breakages were already rehearsed to death. And the one axis where the Railway bleeds (FLEX) is an *adoption away* — process, portable, cheap. The axes where the War Room bleeds (SPEED, REHEARSE) cannot be adopted mid-battle; they must be bought *before* the doors open. Closest fight ever judged. One point. One doctrine.
